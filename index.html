About

I'm a Product Owner / Product Manager based in Cairo with 5+ years in product and 7+ years in tech overall, across B2B/B2C SaaS, EdTech, government platforms and payment-integrated apps.

My sweet spot is the messy middle: turning a vague business goal into a backlog engineers can actually build from, then using data to keep reprioritizing as we learn. Lately that includes scoping AI-powered features and using AI tools myself to move faster from idea to prototype.

Outside work, I mentor PM students, build practical training content for people breaking into product, and stay active in product communities.

  1. Discover

    Talk to users, dig through data, find the real problem.

  2. Define

    Turn that into a clear roadmap and a backlog people trust.

  3. Deliver

    Run the sprints, unblock the team, ship on time.

  4. Measure

    Track it in Amplitude, Mixpanel or SQL to see what's real.

  5. Impact

    Feed it back into the roadmap, then go again.
